Technology and the Future

Discover how technology is changing the world around us.

Introduction

Technology has become an important part of our everyday lives. From smartphones to artificial intelligence, digital tools help us communicate, learn, and solve problems more efficiently.

How Technology Helps Us

Modern technology connects people across the world. Students can attend online classes, businesses can work remotely, and people can access information within seconds.

The Importance of Responsive Design

A responsive webpage adjusts its layout, text, and images to different screen sizes. This makes the website easier to read on mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.

Conclusion

Responsive design creates a better experience for everyone. By using flexible images, readable typography, and suitable content widths, developers can build websites that work well across different devices.
